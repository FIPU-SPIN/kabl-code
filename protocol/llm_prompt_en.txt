BLOOM LEVEL
1 Remember: recalls facts and recognizes them. Verbs: prepoznaje (recognizes), navodi (lists), pronalazi (finds)
2 Understand: understands and expresses in own words. Verbs: objašnjava (explains), opisuje (describes), predviđa (predicts), prikazuje (presents/displays)
3 Apply: carries out a procedure in a familiar or new situation. Verbs: primjenjuje (applies), koristi se (uses), rješava (solves), izvodi (performs)
4 Analyze: breaks into parts and investigates relationships. Verbs: analizira (analyzes), uspoređuje (compares), istražuje (investigates), uočava (notices)
5 Evaluate: makes judgments based on criteria. Verbs: vrednuje (evaluates), procjenjuje (assesses), argumentira (argues), provjerava (checks)
6 Create: assembles new wholes, produces something new. Verbs: stvara (creates), izrađuje (makes), dizajnira (designs), razvija (develops), planira (plans)

Rules for Bloom:
- If an outcome contains several verbs, code the level of the highest verb. Example: "analizira problem, definira ulaz i izlaz, uočava korake" (analyzes the problem, defines input and output, notices steps) contains levels 4 and 2, so the code is 4.
- Look at what the student must actually do according to the outcome, not only at the first verb.
- Outcomes about behaviour and habits (e.g. data protection) are coded with the closest level according to your own judgment, and this is noted in the napomena column.
- For verbs that can be interpreted in two ways (e.g. "uspoređuje", "istražuje", "razlikuje", "odabire"), decide according to your own understanding of the taxonomy and write a note.
